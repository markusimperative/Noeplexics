# Contribution Policy

Noeplexics is currently an author-led research programme. External creative contributions are **not currently accepted**.

## Welcome

Thoughtful issue reports are welcome when they:

- identify a typographical error, broken link, internal inconsistency, or unclear status label;
- ask a focused question about material already present in the repository; or
- report a repository or security problem without including confidential or personal information.

## Not accepted

Please do not submit pull requests, patches, replacement passages, new theory, protocols, diagrams, datasets, software, or other creative material.

Do not submit material that is confidential, proprietary, subject to third-party rights, generated in breach of another agreement, or intended to create an expectation of payment, credit, co-authorship, confidentiality, or ownership.

## Ownership and status

Opening an issue, posting a comment, making a suggestion, or participating in discussion:

- does not transfer ownership to Mark Anil Mathew;
- does not make the participant an author, co-author, collaborator, partner, or joint owner of Noeplexics;
- does not create a confidential, fiduciary, employment, advisory, or compensation relationship; and
- does not make any resulting repository material canonical.

Mark Anil Mathew independently decides whether and how to investigate an issue, revise his work, or change a document's research status.

Any future acceptance of external creative material requires a separate written agreement made before submission. That agreement must expressly define authorship, attribution, permitted use, and intellectual-property ownership.

## Rights

Submitting feedback does not grant permission to reuse repository material. See [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and [AUTHORS.md](AUTHORS.md).
