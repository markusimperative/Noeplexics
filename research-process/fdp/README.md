## Purpose

This pack prepares separate, controlled conversations through which source-derived possibilities can become researcher-controlled foundational decisions. It does not state settled Noeplexics theory, approve any definition or claim, or initiate a publication plan.

The material available here shows part of the decision process: one noncanonical draft conversation module and the four protocols used to explore, challenge, research, and close such decisions. Decision work remains distinct from canonical theory. Nothing in this preview is canonical merely because it is publicly visible.

## Conversation sequence

The conversation sequence is:

```text
Researcher intuition
→ native formulation
→ clarification
→ adversarial pressure
→ research gate
→ targeted deep research
→ post-research reconstruction
→ explicit decision
→ eligibility for canonical drafting
```

## Conducting the conversation

Use the New-Chat Exploration Protocol together with the selected module. The researcher formulates first. The assistant may reflect, clarify, identify assumptions, and progressively introduce adversarial pressure, but it must label its own wording and must not browse before the research gate unless the researcher explicitly requests factual verification.

Rejected, deferred, superseded, and unresolved paths remain part of the provenance record. Lack of correction is not acceptance.

## Research gates

Research begins only after a native formulation, necessity statement, provisional boundaries, explicit non-claims, initial adversarial responses, and concrete research questions have been recorded. Research must answer bounded questions; “research everything related to this” is not an acceptable brief.

External findings must remain distinct from the source-derived Noeplexics position, researcher inference, AI inference, apparent precursors, material differences, and unresolved conflicts.

## Closing or deferring the decision

Use the Conversation Closure Protocol. A conversation may close as approved, provisionally approved, rejected, deferred, or requiring further research. Approval is not mandatory. Canonicalisation permission must be explicit even when the status is approved.

Do not infer a status from conversational fluency, repetition, or silence. The researcher remains the sole authority for acceptance, rejection, revision, deferral, and canonicalisation.

## From decision to canonical drafting

An approved decision becomes only an input eligible for later canonical drafting. Eligibility requires an explicit researcher decision, accepted scope or wording, non-claims, dependencies, objections, disclosed research obligations, and `Canonicalisation permission: Yes` or a bounded `Partial`. A separate task must create or revise canonical material.

This pack must never be treated as settled theory because it contains questions, source-derived possibilities, AI-authored safeguards, and untested research agendas rather than researcher-approved canonical claims.
