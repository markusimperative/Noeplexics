## Gate requirements

Deep research is appropriate only when the conversation has recorded:

- a researcher-native formulation;
- at least one statement explaining why the concept appears necessary;
- provisional boundaries and examples;
- explicit non-claims;
- initial responses to material adversarial objections;
- concrete, bounded research questions.

Research is premature when it would supply the researcher's position, conceal an unresolved question behind an external vocabulary, treat similarity as relevance, or search broadly without a claim at risk.

## Research-question categories

- terminology and prior use;
- philosophical comparison;
- empirical evidence;
- scientific or technical accuracy;
- mathematical validity;
- competing explanations;
- historical precedents;
- ethical and political implications;
- disciplinary interpretation risk.

“Research everything related to this” is not an acceptable research question. Each question must identify why it matters, what proposition or wording is at risk, what evidence would count, and what opposing evidence is required.

## Required provenance separation during later research

```text
Source-derived Noeplexics position
Researcher inference
AI inference
External evidence
External criticism
Apparent precursor
Material difference
Unresolved conflict
```

No external result becomes part of Noeplexics merely because it is relevant, similar, supportive, critical, or elegantly expressed. Research output returns to the researcher for post-research reconstruction.
