# Conversation Closure Protocol

> **Status:** Draft conversation architecture  
> **Canonical status:** Not canonical  
> **Decision status:** Unopened  
> **Intellectual source:** Approved Intellectual Handoff Baseline v0.2  
> **Researcher authority:** Sole authority for acceptance, rejection, revision, deferral, and canonicalisation

A decision conversation may close only when:

- the concept has a bounded working meaning, or an explicit reason it cannot yet be bounded;
- its necessity has been assessed;
- explicit non-claims are recorded;
- major adversarial objections and responses are recorded;
- research has been completed where required, or explicitly deferred with a reason;
- dependencies and downstream effects are recorded;
- the researcher has selected a decision status;
- canonicalisation permission is explicit;
- unresolved issues remain visible;
- provenance categories and rejected paths are preserved.

## Valid closure outcomes

`Approved`, `Provisionally approved`, `Rejected`, `Deferred`, and `Requires further research` are all valid. Closure does not require approval. `Approved` means explicitly accepted for entry into canonical drafting; it does not mean proven, externally validated, original, or already canonical.

## Closure sequence

1. Review the pre-research formulation and every researcher correction.
2. Review the adversarial record and research record.
3. Separate external findings from researcher decisions and AI inferences.
4. Record the accepted formulation, or why no formulation is accepted.
5. Record non-claims, rejected alternatives, dependencies, risks, and unresolved questions.
6. Ask the researcher for the decision status and canonicalisation permission.
7. Obtain a researcher approval statement or an explicit rejection/deferral statement.
8. Create the structured closure record and update the Decision Register.

No canonical file is created or revised by conversation closure alone.
