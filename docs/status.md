# Research Status

Noeplexics uses explicit status labels to separate settled research decisions from working and historical material.

## Canonical

Material is **Canonical** only when Mark Anil Mathew has explicitly approved it as part of canonical Noeplexics theory through a recorded research decision. Coherence, repetition, fluency, public visibility, or lack of correction does not confer canonical status.

## Noncanonical

**Noncanonical** material has not been approved as canonical Noeplexics theory. It may contain questions, source-derived possibilities, AI-proposed wording, co-developed structures, unresolved objections, or untested research agendas.

## Draft

A **Draft** is a working version subject to correction, revision, rejection, or replacement. A draft should not be cited as a settled statement of Noeplexics unless its status and context are made explicit.

## Exploratory

**Exploratory** material structures inquiry. It may elicit native formulations, identify assumptions, propose tests, or preserve competing possibilities without endorsing any of them.

## Superseded

**Superseded** material has been replaced by a later research record but may be retained privately for provenance. Superseded material is not the current programme position.

## Public availability

Public availability does not change research status. A public document remains noncanonical, draft, exploratory, or superseded unless an explicit research decision changes that status.

In this staging set, FDP-01 and the FDP pack materials remain clearly labelled noncanonical drafts. FDP-01 is a conversation module, not canonical theory.
